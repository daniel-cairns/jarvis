4. Rough edges: what makes her feel buggy

  1. FLOW✓ Fixed 4 Nov: talking to her mid-task cancelled the task; typing didn’t. Now a spoken request waits its turn too, and only a redirect (“stop, do X instead”, “no, the Temper one”, “never mind, …”) cancels. A spoken request while she’s busy interrupts her work and starts the new one; the same words typed wait their turn (src/index.ts:572). Saying “Astra, what time is it?” halfway through a teardown throws the teardown away. Fix: queue spoken requests like typed ones, and keep interrupting only for “stop”.
  2. KNOWLEDGE✓ Fixed 4 Nov: what she can do was written down in four places that drifted apart. Now one list (src/abilities.ts) builds the help text, the ui_control description and a “What you can do” section of her instructions, and tests check every phrase in it against her real command matcher. Before: The quick-command patterns (src/ui/control.ts), the help text (src/index.ts:55), the ui_control description (src/tools/server.ts:208) and her instructions (src/prompt.ts). Demo mode was in two of them, so she told you it didn’t exist and paid a session to rebuild it. Fix: one list of her abilities that generates all four.
  3. RESTARTA restart forgets the conversation. Every launch starts fresh unless you say “pick up where we left off”, so a question she asked just before is lost. Fix: after a restart she asked for or that came from a code change, resume the last conversation automatically.
  4. NOISEGod mode announces the session you’re sitting in. It tells you this Claude Code session has finished every time it ends a turn, while you’re looking at it. Fix: skip a session whose window is in front, or only announce once it has waited a few minutes.
  5. DEMOReal background events still reach the screen during the demo. A run’s card, a session refresh or a god-mode announcement can appear in the middle of the tour. Fix: hold everything that isn’t the demo’s own until it ends (the other session’s draft of this is saved as a patch).
  6. VOICEThe voiceprint bar is fitted to you, but untested against anyone else. It now sits just under your own readings; how close other voices score through your mic is unknown. Fix: one test with a second person, reading the score lines in the log.
  7. STATEIdle in god mode looks like “always god mode”. When god mode is on, the status pill shows “GOD MODE” in place of “IDLE” (orb/orb.js:517), so you can’t tell idle from god mode, and the transmission window can still say “Thinking…” after she has finished. Fix: keep the pill for what she’s doing (idle, listening, thinking, speaking) and show god mode separately (next item).
  8. LOOKGod mode barely shows. Its only sign besides the pill is a faint gold glow around the orb (orb/orb.css:306). Fix: a glyph and colour state for it, for example a gold crown glyph on the ring and a gold tint to the core, on top of whatever state she’s in.
  9. SPEEDShe’s slow sometimes. Not measured yet. Likely places: each Claude turn loads every connector (her log lists about 20 MCP servers on every request), the voiceprint model check on each phrase, and Kokoro speech starting. Fix: time each step of a request in her log, then cut the slowest.
  10. IDEAA blue beam to the window she’s working in. While she controls an app, a beam from the orb to that window would show where she’s acting. She already knows the window’s position (her Mac-control tools read it), so it’s a drawing job in the orb.
  11. COSTShe hands work to the session you’re talking in, at full cost. Twice she sent a fix to “the Jarvis session” with auto permissions; once it was this long Claude Code conversation itself, resumed in the background while you were using it, which cost $134.16 for a small change and edited the same files at the same time. Fix: never send to a session that’s open in the app or very long; for her own fixes, start a fresh session with a short brief, and say what it will cost first.
